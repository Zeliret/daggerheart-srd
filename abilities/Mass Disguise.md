# Mass Disguise

**_Level 6_** _Midnight Spell._ **_Recall Cost_** _0._

When you have a few minutes of silence to focus, you can **mark a Stress** to change the appearance of all willing creatures within Close range. Their new forms must share a general body structure and size, and can be somebody or something you've seen before or entirely fabricated. A disguised creature has advantage on **Presence Roll**s to avoid scrutiny.

Activate a Countdown (8). It ticks down as a consequence the GM chooses. When it triggers, the disguise drops.
