# Berserker Initiate

**_Tier 2 Standard._** _A raging warrior who imbibes ritualistic concoctions to enhance their form with aspects of a predatory beast._

- **Motives & Tactics:** Coordinate with packmates, dance around the
- **Difficulty:** 13 | **Thresholds:** 9/18 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 3
- **ATK:** +1 | **Tooth & Claw:** Melee | 2d10 phy
- **Experience:** Hunt +3

### FEATURES

**_Pack Fury:_** The Initiate gains a +1 bonus to their attack rolls for each ally within Very Close range of their attack’s target.

**_Berserker Rage:_** The first time in a scene the Initiate marks their last Stress, they gain a +4 bonus to damage rolls.
