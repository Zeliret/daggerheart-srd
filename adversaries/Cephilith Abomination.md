# Cephilith Abomination

**_Tier 4 Bruiser._** _A lumbering, bog-grown frog beast that has tadpolelike leeches visibly wriggling underneath their translucent skin._

- **Motives & Tactics:** Do the heavy lifting, leap menacingly, move surprisingly fast, smash and grab
- **Difficulty:** 19 | **Thresholds:** 35/70 | **HP:** 7 | **Stress:** 5
- **ATK:** +4 | **Meaty Fists:** Melee | 4d10+10 phy
- **Experience:** Brute +3

### FEATURES

**_Toxic Skin:_** A PC who comes into physical contact with the Abomination must roll a **d6**. On a result of 3 or lower, the PC must **mark an Armor Slot** or 2 HP.

**_Bear Hug:_** **Mark a Stress** to make a standard attack against a target within Melee range. On a success, the target takes standard damage and is Grappled until they escape with a successful **Strength Roll**. While Grappled, the target is _Vulnerable_ and _Restrained_.

**_Crunch!:_** **Spend a Fear** to deal **4d12** direct physical damage to a creature Grappled by the Abomination.

**_Tongue Attack:_** **Spend a Fear** to have the Abomination pull a target within Far range into Melee range, then immediately spotlight the Abomination again.

**_Burrowing Leechpoles:_** When the Abomination takes Major or greater damage, their skin erupts in an explosion of subcutaneous wrigglers. Each PC within Close range must make an **Agility Reaction Roll**. Targets who fail are covered in **2d4** leechpoles, which burrow under their skin. Until the leechpoles are dug out with a successful **Finesse Roll (21)**, any damage the target takes gains a +1 bonus for each leechpole under their skin.
