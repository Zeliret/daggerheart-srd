# Chicken-Foot Hut

**_Tier 2 Bruiser._** _A tall wooden shack that moves around on giant chicken legs._

- **Motives & Tactics:** Claw, protect master, run and jump, trample
- **Difficulty:** 16 | **Thresholds:** 14/27 | **HP:** 8 | **Stress:** 2
- **ATK:** +2 | **Clawed Feet:** Very Close | 2d10+3 phy
- **Experience:** Forests +2

### FEATURES

**_Fast:_** The Hut can move up to Far range when spotlighted.

**_Double Strike:_** **Mark a Stress** to make two standard attacks. If both attacks succeed against the same target, combine the damage.

**_Pin:_** When the Hut makes a successful standard attack, you can **mark a Stress** to temporarily Restrain the target. The Hut drags the _Restrained_ target when it moves.
