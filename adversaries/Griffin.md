# Griffin

**_Tier 4 Standard._** _A large leonine creature that has the head, talons, and wings of an eagle._

- **Motives & Tactics:** Hunt, look for an opening, swoop
- **Difficulty:** 18 | **Thresholds:** 30/45 | **HP:** 6 | **Stress:** 4
- **ATK:** +4 | **Beak & Talons:** Very Close | 4d8+4 phy
- **Experience:** Riches +5, Keen Senses +3

### FEATURES

**_Treasure Hoarder:_** When a PC would mark any number of HP from the Griffi n’s attack, the PC can choose instead to give the Griffi n an equal number of bags of gold.

**_Swooping Slash:_** Move up to Far range and make a standard attack against a target along the way.
