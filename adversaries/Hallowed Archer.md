# Hallowed Archer

**_Tier 4 Ranged._** _Spirit soldiers with sanctified bows._

- **Motives & Tactics:** Focus fire, obey, reposition, volley
- **Difficulty:** 19 | **Thresholds:** 25/45 | **HP:** 3 | **Stress:** 2
- **ATK:** +4 | **Sanctified Longbow:** Far | 4d8+8 phy

### FEATURES

**_Punish the Guilty:_** The Archer deals double damage to targets marked Guilty by a High Seraph.

**_Divine Volley:_** **Mark a Stress** to make a standard attack against up to three targets.
