# Outer Realms Thrall

**_Tier 4 Minion._** _A vaguely humanoid form stripped of memory and identity._

- **Motives & Tactics:** Destroy, disgust, disorient, intimidate
- **Difficulty:** 17 | **Thresholds:** None | **HP:** 1 | **Stress:** 1
- **ATK:** +3 | **Claws and Teeth:** Very Close | 11 phy

### FEATURES

**_Minion (13):_** The Thrall is defeated when they take any damage. For every 13 damage a PC deals to the Thrall, defeat an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

**_Group Attack:_** **Spend a Fear** to choose a target and spotlight all Outer Realm Thralls within Close range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared attack roll. On a success, they deal 11 physical damage each. Combine this damage.
