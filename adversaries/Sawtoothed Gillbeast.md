# Sawtoothed Gillbeast

**_Tier 1 Standard._** _A stocky fish creature that has rows of razor-sharp teeth._

- **Motives & Tactics:** Clamber ashore, hit and run, peek above the
- **Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 5/10 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 2
- **ATK:** +1 | **Waterlogged Weaponry:** Close | 1d8+1 phy
- **Experience:** Aquatic +2

### FEATURES

**_Ka-Chomp:_** Mark a Stress to attack a target within Melee range. On a success, the target takes 1d12 physical damage and must mark an Armor Slot without gaining its benefi t (they can still use armor to reduce the damage).

**_Feeding Frenzy:_** When a creature marks HP, you can spend a Fear to spotlight all Sawtoothed Gillbeasts within Very Close range of them. Those Gillbeasts move into Melee range of the creature and each make a standard attack against them. If any attacks succeed, combine their damage.

**_Scaly:_** When the Gillbeast would mark any number of HP, roll a d6. On a result of 6, you can mark a Stress instead.
