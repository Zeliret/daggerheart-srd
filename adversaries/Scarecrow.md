# Scarecrow

**_Tier 2 Standard._** _A bundle of straw bound into a humanoid shape, clothed in rags, and animated by a trapped soul._

- **Motives & Tactics:** Pull yourself together, scratch and claw
- **Difficulty:** 15 | **Thresholds:** 7/15 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 3
- **ATK:** +1 | **Sickle:** Melee | 2d6+1 phy
- **Experience:** Birds +2, Fields +2

### FEATURES

**_Made of Straw:_** The Scarecrow has resistance to physical damage but takes double damage from fire.

**_Terror Vision:_** **Spend a Fear** to have the Scarecrow lock eyes with a PC within Very Close range, dealing **2d8** magic damage and making them _Vulnerable_ until they roll with Hope.
