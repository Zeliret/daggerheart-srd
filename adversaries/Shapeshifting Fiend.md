# Shapeshifting Fiend

**_Tier 3 Social._** _An evil entity who impersonates a trusted figure._

- **Motives & Tactics:** Impersonate, sow discord, tempt betrayal
- **Difficulty:** 17 | **Thresholds:** 20/32 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 8
- **ATK:** +3 | **Hidden Blade:** Melee | 3d8+3 phy
- **Experience:** Manipulate +3, Mimic +2

### FEATURES

**_Fiendish Nature:_** The Fiend has resistance to magic damage and can’t be fooled by magic illusions.

**_Impersonate:_** Spend a Fear to have the Fiend magically transform into a humanoid they have previously interacted with. A PC who targets the Fiend while they’re in the form of a person the PC cares for must succeed on an Instinct Reaction Roll or the attack fails and the PC must mark a Stress.

**_Turn Invisible:_** Mark a Stress to make the Fiend Hidden until they make an action roll or take damage.

**_Backhanded Guidance:_** When a PC makes an action roll while the Fiend is in the scene, you can mark a Stress to add 1d6 to the result of their Fear Die.

**_Exposed! (Phase Change):_** When the Fiend is defeated or the PCs discover its true identity, replace the Fiend with Shapeshifting Fiend Revealed and immediately spotlight them.
