# Spellbound Armor

**_Tier 1 Bruiser._** _An empty suit of full plate armor that glows with a faint corona of ghostly light._

- **Motives & Tactics:** Clank noisily, move like clockwork, watch
- **Difficulty:** 10 | **Thresholds:** 9/17 | **HP:** 6 | **Stress:** None
- **ATK:** +0 | **Zweihänder:** Very Close | 1d10+1 phy
- **Experience:** Guardian +2

### FEATURES

**_Tireless:_** The Armor can’t be forced to mark Stress. When an eff ect would cause it to mark Stress, the Armor ignores that part of the eff ect.

**_Clatter & Recombobulate:_** Once per scene when the Armor is defeated, you can spend a Fear to reactivate it. Clear 2 HP and immediately spotlight it.
