# Viper

**_Tier 1 Minion._** _A small serpent that envenomates its prey with hinged fangs._

- **Motives & Tactics:** Lie in wait, slither, strike
- **Difficulty:** 11 | **Thresholds:** None | **HP:** 1 | **Stress:** 1
- **ATK:** +3 | **Bite:** Melee | 1 phy
- **Experience:** Grounded +4

### FEATURES

**_Minion (4):_** The Viper is defeated when it takes any damage. For every 4 damage a PC deals to the Viper, the PC defeats an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

**_Venomous:_** When the Viper makes a successful standard attack, the target becomes temporarily Envenomated. While Envenomated, the target must **mark a Stress** whenever they make an action roll.

**_Group Attack:_** **Spend a Fear** to choose a target and spotlight all Vipers within Close range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared attack roll. On a success, they deal 1 physical damage each. Combine this damage.
