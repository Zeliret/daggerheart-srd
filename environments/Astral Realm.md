# Astral Realm

**_Tier 3 Traversal._** _chic realm of energy and emotion, where the strong in spirit can reshape the rules of reality._

- **Impulses:** Attack their psyches, summon their subconscious fears, warp their perceptions
- **Difficulty:** 16
- **Potential Adversaries:** [Demon of Despair](../adversaries/Demon%20Of%20Despair.md), [Elemental Spark](../adversaries/Elemental%20Spark.md), [Pain Priest](../adversaries/Pain%20Priest.md), Psychic [Vampires](../adversaries/Vampire.md) ([Head Vampire](../adversaries/Head%20Vampire.md), [Night Children](../adversaries/Night%20Children.md))

### FEATURES

**_Out-of-Body Experience - Passive:_** Because the party isn’t physically in this realm, Strength, Agility, and **Finesse Roll**s automatically fail. Additionally, when a character would mark a HP, they must lose a Hope or **mark a Stress** instead. If a character can’t **mark a Stress**, they must make a death move. _How does the projection of your mental self-image differ from your physical appearance? Which one do you consider the “real” you?_

**_Mental Manifestations - Passive:_** With a successful **Knowledge Roll**, PCs can alter the psychic environment, such as by inverting gravity or conjuring a rapidly self-assembling staircase. A PC can use a mental manifestation once per scene to gain advantage on a roll to navigate the Astral Realm. _Is the construct you manifest a memory from real life or a figment of your own imagination? What key detail do you focus on to make it more stable?_

**_Matter Is Illusion - Passive:_** Countdown (10). Time and distance are relative here, and simply moving through the space requires an act of will. To cross the Astral Realm, the party must complete the countdown using only Knowledge, Instinct, or **Presence Roll**s. On a failure with Fear, a PC takes **3d8** direct magic damage. Do you think or feel your way through this place? _What difference does it make here?_

**_Astral Adversaries - Passive:_** Adversaries in the Astral Realm are immune to physical damage, as they are psychic projections instead of flesh and blood. _How does an adversary’s astral form differ from their physical form? Why are these psychic projections drawn to the PCs?_
