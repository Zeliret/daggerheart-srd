# Transformations

Transformations are a new character option introduced in Daggerheart SRD 2.0. Each Transformation provides a powerful benefit paired with a meaningful drawback. A character can have only one Transformation at a time, and Transformation cards do not count toward the character's loadout maximum.

The six SRD 2.0 Transformations are:

- [Demigod](Demigod.md)
- [Ghost](Ghost.md)
- [Reanimated](Reanimated.md)
- [Shapeshifter](Shapeshifter.md)
- [Vampire](Vampire.md)
- [Werewolf](Werewolf.md)

See the individual Transformation entries for their complete rules and limitations.
