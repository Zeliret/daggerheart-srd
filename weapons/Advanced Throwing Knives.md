# Advanced Throwing Knives

**_Tier 3_** _Secondary_ _Physical_ _Weapon_

- **Trait:** Finesse
- **Range:** Melee
- **Damage:** d8+4 phy
- **Burden:** One-Handed

### FEATURE

**_Stockpiled:_** You can throw this weapon within Close range by making an attack roll using Finesse. You don’t have to retrieve it, as you always have another on hand.
