# Bladed Star

**_Tier 3_** _Primary_ _Physical_ _Weapon_

- **Trait:** Knowledge
- **Range:** Close
- **Damage:** d8+7 phy
- **Burden:** One-Handed

### FEATURE

**_Rebounding:_** When you throw this weapon, it returns to your hand. On a failed attack, you can reroll your attack with disadvantage.
