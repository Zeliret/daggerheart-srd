# Eldritch Vambrace

**_Tier 2_** _Secondary_ _Magical_ _Weapon_

- **Trait:** Instinct
- **Range:** Melee
- **Damage:** d8 mag
- **Burden:** One-Handed

### FEATURE

**_Deflecting:_** When you are attacked, you can mark an Armor Slot to gain a bonus to your Evasion equal to your Armor Score against the attack.
