# Gravity Arbalest

**_Tier 4_** _Primary_ _Magical_ _Weapon_

- **Trait:** Knowledge
- **Range:** Far
- **Damage:** d8+6 mag
- **Burden:** Two-Handed

### FEATURE

**_Magnetic:_** When you make an attack with this weapon, you can spend a Hope to force all adversaries within Very Close range of the target to make a Reaction Roll (16). Creatures who fail must mark a Stress and are pulled into Melee range of the target.
