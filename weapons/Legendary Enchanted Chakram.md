# Legendary Enchanted Chakram

**_Tier 4_** _Primary_ _Magical_ _Weapon_

- **Trait:** Finesse
- **Range:** Close
- **Damage:** d6+10 mag
- **Burden:** One-Handed

### FEATURE

**_Ricochet:_** When you throw this weapon, it returns to your hand. When you make an attack, you can mark a Stress to target another creature within Very Close range of the first target with that attack.
