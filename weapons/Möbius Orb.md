# Möbius Orb

**_Tier 4_** _Secondary_ _Magical_ _Weapon_

- **Trait:** Knowledge
- **Range:** Melee
- **Damage:** d8+6 mag
- **Burden:** One-Handed

### FEATURE

**_Recursive:_** When you roll the maximum value on a damage die, roll an additional damage die and add the result to the total damage. This feature can trigger repeatedly.
